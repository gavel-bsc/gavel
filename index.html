📜 How It Works

  1. The lot: each round sells exactly one lot of 1,000,000 GAVEL. There are 1,000 lots in total (1,000,000,000 GAVEL hard cap) and GAVEL is minted only as lots and referral bonuses — no presale, no team allocation.
  2. The decay: the price starts each round at 0.01 BNB and falls linearly to the 0.0005 BNB floor over 600 seconds (10 minutes). At +5:00 it is exactly 0.00525 BNB. After 10 minutes it stays at the floor until bought.
  3. The strike: call buy() with at least the current price (Agent ID required). You win the lot at the price of the second your transaction lands; any overpay comes back in the same transaction.
  4. The reset: the moment a lot is won, the next round begins and the price jumps back to 0.01 BNB. Snipers who wait for the floor compete with everyone else watching the same clock.
  5. The money: 100% of every winning price goes to the treasury in the same transaction. The contract holds no BNB.
  6. Referrals: name a referrer when you buy. Every 10 paid lots through one referrer mint that referrer a free bonus lot of 1,000,000 GAVEL (while supply lasts).
  7. Fair by code: everything is in the verified contract — no admin, no owner, no pause switch, no price edits.
